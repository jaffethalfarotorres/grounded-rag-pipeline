# Service level agreement

Acme Cloud targets 99.9% monthly uptime for every paid plan. Enterprise workspaces target 99.95% monthly uptime.

## Service credits

A service credit of 10% of the monthly fee applies when monthly uptime falls below the target. The credit rises to 25% when monthly uptime falls below 99.0%.

Credit requests must be sent within 30 days of the incident.

## Maintenance

Scheduled maintenance is announced at least 72 hours in advance and does not count as downtime.
